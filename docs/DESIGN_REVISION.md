# Reference-led design revision

The owner directly requested a closer reproduction of https://www.dalbergdesign.com/ after seeing the first build. The live reference was inspected at desktop size, including its homepage and service-page compositions.

Applied to Mirror Metrics:
- A white, wide header with a left-aligned company identity and five uppercase navigation links.
- A full-width photographic hero, approximately 520px high on desktop, with a bold white paragraph-scale heading.
- Open Sans for body/hero text and Raleway for headings/navigation, self-hosted through next/font.
- A centered 960px content column, approximately 39px bold headings, regular body copy, and thin section rules.
- A pale brand-color purpose banner, an original research-process graphic, three-column illustrative application cards, two-column context stories, and a simpler image/text partnership section.
- Consistent photo banners on About, Services, Sectors, Approach, and Research Applications.
- A flat, compact treatment for article cards, forms, and footer navigation.

The existing Mirror Metrics logo and licensed Bangladesh photo library are retained. No reference-site photographs, proprietary illustrations, source code, staff, client lists, project results, or contact details were imported. Application cards remain explicitly illustrative and all original content routes remain available.
