## Boundaries and launch review

Pexels permits free reuse and modification, including commercial use, subject to its terms. Attribution is not required by that license; these credits are retained for traceability. No individual model or property release documents were independently obtained. A stock copyright license does not establish every other right, so the owner should review identifiable people, signs, and the intended promotional context before public launch, or substitute owner-cleared photography. No person pictured is represented as a Mirror Metrics employee, client, participant, or endorser. The footer explicitly describes the images as contextual photography.

The portrait illustrates a perspective about people and inclusion; it is not evidence that the subject is an entrepreneur or a research participant. The current library does not independently document a women-led enterprise, a research discussion, or a health/education setting. Those subjects should be added using owner-cleared photographs if exact visual coverage is required. A paddy image originally considered (Pexels 29418729) was unavailable for download and was replaced by the separately verified harvest image above; it is not included or credited as a delivered asset.
